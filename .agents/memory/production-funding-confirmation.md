---
name: Production funding confirmation
description: Boundary between production approval and accounting transactions.
---

An approved production plan asks finance to confirm that its estimated funds are available. Finance confirmation is an operational gate for scheduling, not a reservation, payment, transfer, or accounting entry.

**Why:** Approval and availability do not prove that cash moved or a payable arose. Recording a journal at either step would misstate the ledger and risk a second entry when the actual supplier transaction occurs.

**How to apply:** Keep the planning and finance-review statuses distinct from purchasing, bank transfers, supplier invoices, and posted journals. If real commitments or disbursements are added later, model and approve them as separate financial events.
