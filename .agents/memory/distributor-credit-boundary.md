---
name: Distributor credit boundary
description: Business meaning of distributor order submission and approved contract credit limits
---

Submitting a distributor request is not an invoice, debt, payment, stock reservation, or shipment. Manual approval may issue one company invoice, but must not automatically collect or send an external shipment.

Credit approval belongs to explicitly reviewed contract terms, while debt exposure belongs to the company across all its contracts. Changing the active contract must not discard older unpaid balances. A missing approved limit must block current issuance; it never means unlimited credit.

**Why:** The user explicitly required these boundaries for the distributor portal and required the same protection on existing current company-invoice issuance paths.

**How to apply:** Preserve these rules in every new distributor ordering or company billing path. Do not infer limits from scanned text or silently modify signed documents. Keep historical records unchanged.
