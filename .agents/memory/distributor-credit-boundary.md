---
name: Distributor credit boundary
description: Business meaning of distributor order submission and approved contract credit limits
---

Submitting a distributor request is not an invoice, debt, payment, stock reservation, or shipment. Manual approval may issue one company invoice, but must not automatically collect or send an external shipment.

Credit approval belongs to explicitly reviewed contract terms, while debt exposure belongs to the company across all its contracts. Changing the active contract must not discard older unpaid balances. The owner's revised rule allows direct admin company/distributor invoice issuance without an approved limit; when a limit is approved, keep enforcing it. Distributor-portal credit-controlled approval must remain unchanged without separate owner confirmation.

**Why:** The owner revised the no-approved-limit rule for admin invoice issuance, then explicitly confirmed separating that rule from distributor-portal approval after reviewing the shared path. Portal submission and approval must remain unchanged.

**How to apply:** Distinguish direct admin issuance from portal order approval even when they share invoice code. Preserve approval identity, time, reason and audit history. Do not infer limits from scanned text, invent performance-based limits, or silently modify signed documents. Keep historical records unchanged.
