---
name: Owner-paid purchases
description: Accounting treatment for business purchases paid personally by the owner.
---

Record a business purchase paid from the owner’s personal account as an expense or direct purchase debit and a credit to the owner payable/current-account liability. Do not credit company cash or bank, and do not treat the payment as equity.

**Why:** The company received the benefit but did not spend company funds, so it owes the owner until a separate reimbursement is recorded.

**How to apply:** Use this treatment for every owner-account purchase. When the company reimburses the owner, create a separate settlement entry that debits the owner liability and credits the company bank account.
