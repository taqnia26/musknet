---
name: Standalone individual invoice intent
description: Why direct individual invoices must remain independent of orders and distributor invoicing.
---

The user chose **«فاتورة مباشرة»**, not **«طلب ثم فاتورة عند السداد»**, for individual invoice creation.

**Why:** The user specifically asked to add invoices from the Individual Invoices page and confirmed the option that does not create an order or shipment.

**How to apply:** Keep direct individual issuance independent of the storefront order and distributor-contract workflows. Do not replace it with a link to order creation when improving the invoice screen.

The buyer's phone belongs only to the invoice snapshot, not to a linked shopper account.

**Why:** The user chose «حفظ الجوال داخل الفاتورة فقط» after being offered existing-customer lookup and customer creation alternatives.

**How to apply:** Capture and display the phone on new standalone invoices without creating, matching, merging, or changing shopper accounts. Do not infer phone numbers for historical invoices.
