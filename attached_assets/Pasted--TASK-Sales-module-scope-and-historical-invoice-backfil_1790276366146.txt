# TASK — Sales module: scope and historical invoice backfill

## 1. Scope the Sales section to invoicing only
The Sales section should contain only: issuing invoices, collecting/recording payment against invoices, and uploading invoices for individuals and companies. Audit everything currently under the Sales menu and report anything outside this scope before removing or moving it — do not delete or relocate anything without confirming it first, since some of it may be relied on elsewhere.

## 2. Backfill historical distributor invoices
Add a way to record distributor/company invoices that were already issued earlier this year outside the system (so this year's full invoice history is in the system), with:
- Entry of the invoice's real original details (number, date, buyer, items, amounts) rather than forcing today's date or a new auto-generated number where a real historical one already exists.
- A collection-status option per invoice supporting partial collection — some of the invoice amount collected, some still outstanding — not just a binary paid/unpaid flag.

This must not bypass the accounting and invoice-integrity rules already in place for normal invoice creation (unique creation key/idempotency, the single-invoice-source constraint, correct journal postings for revenue and any payments recorded against it, correct sequence-number handling so backfilled invoices don't collide with or corrupt the live sequence). Report your approach for keeping backfilled invoices consistent with these constraints before implementing.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Report of anything under Sales outside the stated scope, with no removal until confirmed
- [ ] A historical distributor invoice can be entered with its real original number/date/amounts
- [ ] Partial collection against a backfilled invoice works and is reflected correctly in outstanding balance and accounting
- [ ] Backfilled invoices do not break invoice sequence numbering or accounting integrity
